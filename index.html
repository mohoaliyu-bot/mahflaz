The Company

A Generational
Nigerian Enterprise

Incorporated on 4 March 2008 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act, Mahflaz Concepts Limited is a private limited liability company headquartered in Abuja, Federal Capital Territory. With over seventeen years of continuous operations, the company has built a credible footprint across general commodities trading, industrial product distribution, oil and gas services, and corporate investment.

We combine deep market knowledge of the Nigerian and West African corridor with a rigorous, compliance-led approach to trading and distribution. Our registered office is at 28 Arusha Crescent, Off Nouakchott Street, Zone 1, Wuse, Abuja — and the company holds Active status with the Corporate Affairs Commission.

Our Business

Four Pillars of
Commercial Activity

In line with our Memorandum and Articles of Association, Mahflaz Concepts Limited is empowered to carry on a broad spectrum of commercial activities. Our operational focus is concentrated in four interconnected areas, each reinforcing the other as part of a coherent long-term strategy.

Commodities Trading & Distribution

The commercial heart of Mahflaz. We operate as a structured off-take and distribution platform for Nigeria’s leading manufacturers — providing assured market reach, disciplined route-to-market execution, and reliable downstream evacuation for finished goods and industrial commodities.

Our focus spans refined food commodities, industrial raw materials, agro-industrial inputs, and consumer packaged goods — markets where our logistics infrastructure, buyer networks, and compliance standing create lasting competitive advantage.

  • Bulk off-take and distribution on a sustained, scheduled basis
  • Warehousing across Abuja, Lagos, Kano & Port Harcourt corridors
  • Haulage logistics for dry goods and bulk liquid commodity handling
  • Cash-and-carry model compliant with principal manufacturer terms
  • NAFDAC & SON quality assurance for food-grade commodities
  • Sub-distributor networks across wholesale and institutional buyers

Oil & Gas Services

Mahflaz operates in Nigeria’s oil and gas sector as a trading, logistics coordination, and procurement support entity — serving both upstream and downstream operators with a compliance-led approach aligned to NMDPRA and NUPRC standards.

Our mandate under the Memorandum of Association explicitly covers Oil & Gas and allied products, providing a strong legal foundation for our sector activities.

  • Trading and supply of petroleum products, lubricants, and industrial inputs
  • Brokerage and logistics coordination for upstream / downstream operators
  • Procurement support aligned with NMDPRA / NUPRC compliance
  • Agent and representative services for international energy companies
  • Strategic procurement for oilfield service inputs and chemicals

Corporate Investment

Long-term capital deployment is central to our strategy. We pursue strategic equity participation in Nigerian operating companies across agro-industry, energy, and real assets — bringing both capital and commercial relationships to portfolio companies.

Our investment mandate includes co-investment and SPV structuring with international investors, development finance institutions, and strategic industrial partners aligned with Nigeria’s growth agenda.

  • Strategic equity participation in agro-industry, energy, and real assets
  • Sponsoring of Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) for project execution
  • Partnership structuring with international investors and DFIs
  • Co-investment in manufacturing and agro-processing assets
  • Support for Nigeria’s import-substitution and backward integration
  • Letter of Credit, Bank Guarantee, and Bid Bond facilities available

Strategic Outlook

Our Roadmap
for Growth

Mahflaz is positioning itself as an enduring commercial platform and investment vehicle for the long term. Our medium-term strategy focuses on five priority areas — each building on our established compliance infrastructure, ownership model, and commercial relationships across Nigeria and West Africa.

Priority 1 · Now
Strengthen Core Distribution Relationships

Formalise structured off-take and distribution agreements with principal manufacturers across key commodity categories — building long-term recurring revenue streams anchored on compliance, logistics reliability, and established buyer networks.

Priority 2 · 2025–2026
Proprietary Logistics Infrastructure

Invest in warehousing and bulk logistics capacity across key distribution corridors — reducing third-party logistics dependence, improving unit economics, and enabling faster route-to-market execution for manufacturer partners.

Priority 3 · 2026–2027
ECOWAS Regional Expansion

Extend distribution and investment coverage into Niger, Benin, and Cameroon — leveraging the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) framework to access regional markets and create new routes for Nigerian manufacturers.

Priority 4 · 2026–2028
Manufacturing & Agro-Industrial Co-Investments

Develop co-investment opportunities in manufacturing plants, agro-processing facilities, and industrial assets across Nigeria — supporting import-substitution, creating vertically integrated value chains, and generating long-term capital growth within the portfolio.

Ongoing
Digital Infrastructure & ESG Commitment

Strengthen ERP, CRM, and supply chain visibility platforms across the group. Deliver measurable ESG targets including carbon and waste reduction across logistics operations, and deepen community engagement programmes in host areas.

Ethics & Integrity

Corporate
Commitments

Mahflaz Concepts Limited conducts business according to the highest moral, ethical, and legal standards. Our formal policies govern all employees, directors, officers, and third-party partners — across every geography in which we operate.

🤝
Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy

Zero-tolerance on bribery and corrupt practices in all forms. Employees are prohibited from offering, promising, or accepting payments to or from government officials or private entities to secure improper advantage. Third parties must adhere to the same standard and document all interactions on behalf of the Company. Violations are subject to civil, criminal, and disciplinary penalties.

Effective 11 February 2024
💚
Anti-Slavery & Human Trafficking Policy

Zero-tolerance on modern slavery in all its forms — including slavery, servitude, forced and compulsory labour, and human trafficking. Our commitment extends throughout supply chains and to all contractors, suppliers, and business partners. All employees receive induction training on identification and reporting. Suppliers are contractually required to maintain equivalent standards.

Effective 11 February 2024
